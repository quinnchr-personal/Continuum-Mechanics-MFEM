Flux-Kernel Multiphysics Architecture

An MFEM-based framework for nonlinear solid mechanics, heat and mass transfer, thermo-chemistry, and compressible flow — CG, DG, and HDG from one physics definition, portable across MPI + CUDA/HIP/OpenMP.

The contracts the arrows stand for

  • physics → seam. A module contributes F(u,∇u), S(u), F̂(u⁻,u⁺,n) and boundary conditions — never an integrator. Solid mechanics fits the same shape: F = P(F) in total Lagrangian form.
  • materials → seam. Constitutive models are stateless device functors; internal variables (pyrolysis extent, plastic strain, char fraction) live in QuadratureFunctions so they survive GPUs and checkpointing. Tangents come from forward-mode dual numbers unless hand-optimized.
  • seam → kernels. The framework turns one definition into CG volume, DG face, or HDG trace operators; partial assembly for GPU operator action, full assembly when a matrix is needed for AMG or direct solves.
  • ALE. A wrapper applied to any flux on the fixed referent configuration — substitute the mapped flux and weight by J — not a per-physics rewrite.
